CLOSEDQUORUM is a Windows malware sample that polls multiple commercial LLM providers and uses their decisions to select its next actions, without requiring ongoing operator tasking. The reporting describes evidence connecting the sample's developer to criminal forum activity, while noting that deployment in the wild has not been confirmed.
