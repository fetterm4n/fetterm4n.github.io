VOLTA-AUTODOXER combines traditional infostealer capabilities with an AI-assisted victim profiling stage. After collecting credentials, sessions, and other personal data, it sends a structured evidence bundle to language models to produce a victim assessment and rank potentially monetizable assets. The research was identified through the CAIRN framework.
